# Data sources, licences and attribution

Personal desk study. SHA-256 hashes of every raw file read are in `data/inputs/raw_sources.csv`, and of every committed input in `data/inputs/manifest.csv` (hashes of the LF-normalised bytes). Raw downloads live in `data/raw/` (git-ignored) and are fetched again by `scripts/fetch_data.py`; `scripts/prepare_inputs.py` turns them into the committed inputs in `data/inputs/`.

## Synthetic and illustrative

- `results/survey_synthetic_respondents.csv` is SYNTHETIC: simulated households generated by `stm/survey.py` from the model's population and trip tables. **No travel survey was carried out and no real respondent data exist in this repository.**
- The scheme costs in `config/appraisal.json` are ILLUSTRATIVE placeholders with no source. No total cost of the Jurong Region Line was found in a public source, and LTA's individual contract awards were not added up.
- Not found in public sources, and therefore assumed or left out: a Singapore public-sector discount rate and appraisal period; Singapore vehicle operating costs; an accident rate per vehicle-km by road type; a social cost of carbon.

## Not used

- No traffic counts (there are no public link-level counts for Singapore's roads).
- No LTA DataMall train origin-destination data, traffic speed bands or traffic images.
- No DataMall AccountKey is stored in this repository or used by its scripts.
